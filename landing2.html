The process

Selling is a three-step job.

No listings fees. No complicated setup. Post in under a minute and start getting messages.

1

Post your listing

Snap a photo, set your price, pick a category. Your listing goes live immediately and is reviewed for quality automatically.

2

Chat with buyers

Interested buyers message you directly inside the app. No phone numbers shared until you decide to. Real-time, private, secure.

3

Trade locally

Meet in person, complete the deal, and leave a review. That's it. No middlemen, no service fees, no nonsense.
